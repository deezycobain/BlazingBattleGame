# Attack Targeting Visual Standard — v0.6.3

## Normal attacks
All standard/basic attacks now use the same targeting language:
- white range field
- white target/hit bubble
- white target outline

This applies regardless of character.

Lebee remains mechanically ranged:
- her Star Blast keeps its long rectangular forward lane
- only the presentation changed from cosmic-magenta to white

## Jutsu
Jutsu targeting uses move/character color:
- Lebee Meteor Jutsu: cosmic pink/magenta
- Sub-Zero Freeze Blast: ice cyan/blue
- Crimson Jutsu: warm crimson/red

Link Attack highlighting remains green when active.

No hitbox geometry, damage, stats, animations, team selection, or battle logic changed.
