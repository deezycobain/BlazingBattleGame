# Lebee Meteor Jutsu — v0.5.31

Presentation-only upgrade built on the v0.5.30 data-authority architecture.

- Target shape: full battlefield rectangle (`screen_rect`)
- Cast: ~1.05 s, intentionally slower than the meteor shower
- Screen dim: 42% maximum during cast/shower
- Meteors: 12, distributed across the screen and converging on valid targets
- Meteor visual scale: large at entry, shrinking toward impact
- Damage, ATK scaling, chakra cost and stats remain canonical in `assets/characters/lebee/data/unit.json`
