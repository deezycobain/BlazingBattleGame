# Senku Revival Formula — v0.6.12

Senku's Jutsu is now a support/healing ability.

## Targeting
- targets allied player units only
- enemies are excluded from Jutsu hit detection
- all living active player units receive a green healing highlight during preview

## Effect
- costs 4 chakra
- heals every living active player unit directly to full HP
- deals zero enemy damage
- no enemy recoil or status effect

## Animation
The existing green bottle Jutsu sheet remains in use:
prepare -> raise bottle -> throw -> green bottle -> green impact/smoke.

The projectile lands at the center of the allied team and the heal resolves on impact.

Normal Senku bomb attack remains unchanged.
