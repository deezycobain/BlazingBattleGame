- Player renderer now consumes each unit's canonical `render.scale`.
- Scale applies to idle, attack, recoil, Jutsu body frames, and grounded shadow.

- Stats, hitboxes, movement, damage, Freeze Blast projectile size, and combat logic are unchanged.
