SUB-ZERO JUTSU ASSET STATUS — v0.5.16

The supplied SubZero_Sprite_Assets package contains:
- Idle
- Walk 4-frame sheet
- Basic Hit
- Recoil 4-frame sheet
- Reference

It does NOT contain a dedicated Jutsu/casting animation or ice-attack frame.
The runtime therefore no longer substitutes the Basic Hit punch for Jutsu.
Until a Jutsu pose/animation is supplied, Sub-Zero keeps his normalized idle body
art during the Jutsu lunge/cast and the existing blue Jutsu hitbox/impact treatment
handles gameplay feedback.
